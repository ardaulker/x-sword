In this test bench humans take turns on the same phone. Online rooms live in the game itself.

    Rules

    • Goal and points: The match ends when one star is left. The score decides the winner: taking a player is 50, the Twin 30, a bot 10 points; the star left standing gets a +30 bonus. Ranking: score, then number of takes, then survival time. Hiding alone doesn't win.
    • Stars: 2–4 players, each with one star. An AI player takes an empty seat; its edge is dashed. Every match the stars start from random corners and the bots in a random layout.
    • Board: 9×9 for 2 players, 11×11 for 3, 13×13 for 4.
    • Mode: Every round is either STRAIGHT or DIAGONAL and it flips every round. Stars move and take in that direction.
    • Red bot: walks straight, takes diagonally. Blue bot: walks diagonally, takes straight.
    • Move: Everyone moves one square. Taking means moving onto the target's square. Anyone can take anyone.
    • Order: Shuffled once at the start and kept all match. Stars and bots play in this one queue; a piece's number is its place among that round's survivors and is reassigned every round (the order doesn't change). A taken piece's turn is skipped.
    • Bonuses: Everyone starts with one double step. Bonuses are earned by conditions: 20 points double step, 40 points double move, 60 points one of the two; 50 points Switcheroo (swap places with any piece on the board); surviving the first shrink gives armor (you can't be taken once); surviving the second gives a double move; in single player a take by the Twin also gives a Switcheroo and double points. In the arena bonuses are only earned; using them is in the game.
    • The arena shrinks: Every 6 rounds the outer ring collapses at the end of the round. The ring is flagged one round before and striped during the collapse round. Pieces left on it fall.
    • Colors: green your paths, orange the tapped piece's paths, red squares where a take happens.
    • Preview: Tap a square. Pieces that could take you there light up with a red frame. Tap Confirm or the same square again.
    • Bots: Easy — takes if it can, otherwise walks to the nearest star. Normal — never steps where it would be taken, never stays on a collapsing ring, heads for the star it can reach fastest. Hard — on top of that, closes its target's escape squares.

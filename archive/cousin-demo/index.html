Game Rules

  • You (Green Star ★): You are in the middle of the board. Every turn your move mode switches between STRAIGHT and DIAGONAL.
  • Dark Twin (Green with a Black Dot [1]): Follows exactly the same rules as you. It changes form just like you.
  • Green Forms: As a square you move and take straight. As a diamond you move and take diagonally.
  • Red Bots (Square): They only walk STRAIGHT, but only take DIAGONALLY. (Their swords are diagonal.)
  • Blue Bots (Diamond): They only walk DIAGONALLY, but only take STRAIGHT. (Their swords are straight.)
  • Colors: Green squares are your paths. Tap a bot and the orange squares show its paths. Red squares are pieces that can be taken.
  • Order: You move first, then the bots move by the numbers 1 to 17 on the pieces. The direction of the swords always shows who can take where!

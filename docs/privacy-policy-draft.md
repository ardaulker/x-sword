# Privacy policy: DRAFT for review

Status: a draft written from how the game works today. It is not published. It needs Arda's contact details, a legal
read-through and a decision about analytics before the app stores ask for it (a public URL is required).

Facts the text rests on (check them again before publishing):

- No account, no X Sword server, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Fonts are bundled (no request to Google).
- Stored on the device only: settings, statistics, puzzle stars, the paused match, a profile (name, color, random id).
- Multiplayer connects phones directly (WebRTC) through the free PeerJS signaling server. That server sees the room
  code and the IP addresses used to connect; the other players in the room can see your IP address through the direct
  connection. Only your profile name and color are sent to them.
- The app stores' native sign-in (Game Center, Google Play Games) will add the platform's own player id and cloud save;
  that data is handled by Apple and Google under their own policies.

## X Sword privacy policy

**Last updated:** <date>

**Who we are.** X Sword is made by <name>. Contact: <email>.

**What the game stores on your device.** Your settings, statistics, puzzle progress, the match you paused, and a profile
(a display name, a color and a random id). This stays on your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's data.

**What we collect.** Nothing. X Sword has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we run no server that receives your
data.

**Multiplayer.** To play with friends, your phone connects directly to theirs. To set the connection up, the game uses the
public PeerJS service, which sees the room code and the network addresses involved. The players in your room can see
your IP address through the direct connection and receive your display name and color. Do not share a room code with
people you do not want to play with.

**Sign-in with Game Center / Google Play Games** (phone apps only). If you sign in, Apple or Google provides your player
name and id and stores your cloud save under their own privacy policies.

**Children.** The game collects no personal data, so it can be played by children. <age rating to be set>.
